### Modifications made in this project

The public MARS data were:

- downloaded and cryptographically hashed for provenance;
- parsed and normalized in Python;
- checked for schema and structural QA/QC conditions;
- assigned record-level QA flags and PASS/WARN/FAIL status;
- represented as WGS84 point geometries from published latitude/longitude fields;
- aggregated into tables and figures;
- summarized in an independent technical report.

No methane retrieval, radiative-transfer calculation, plume-detection model, or independent emission quantification is performed.
